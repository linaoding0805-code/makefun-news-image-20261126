## 6. Column 3 — "GAZA STRIKES KILL NINE" — PARTIAL (undercount; update to 10)
- The page's "at least nine… overnight" matches the EARLY Reuters wire, 26 Nov 2026
  ("killed at least nine Palestinians in Gaza overnight, medics said, including the
  Hamas-appointed deputy minister of education… third consecutive night of air
  attacks despite a one-year-old ceasefire"; Abu Nada and his wife killed in
  Deir al-Balah).
- But the toll was updated the same day: the later Reuters report, 26 Nov 2026,
  says "at least 10 people in Gaza"; AP, 26 Nov 2026, reports "Five Israeli strikes
  and a shooting overnight and on Thursday killed 10 Palestinians across Gaza,
  according to officials at Shifa and Al-Aqsa Martyrs hospitals" (Abu Nada + wife,
  seven killed in three other strikes, one 62-year-old shot in Bureij). Two
  independent sources agree on 10 for the full Thursday toll.
- Decision: the page should carry **10**, not 9. The early "nine overnight" figure
  was superseded before the day ended, and a front page summarising 26 Nov should
  use the full-day toll. The framing must also change from "overnight" to
  "overnight and on Thursday".
- Precision point: Abu Nada was the "Hamas-appointed deputy minister of education"
  (Reuters) / deputy minister of education and economy and a member of Hamas's
  political office (NY Post / Hamas confirmation, 26 Nov 2026). "Hamas deputy
  minister" is loose; use the fuller title.
Corrections 2 and 3 apply.

  ("SpaceX said on Wednesday…", CEOWORLD/Parade/Reuters, 26 Nov 2026), with Musk's
  X post timestamped 26 Nov (UTC). It is therefore borderline for a 26 Nov page:
  defensible only as a 26 Nov report of a late-25 Nov announcement, and only with
